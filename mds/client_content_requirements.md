# Cyber-I website content to request from the client

The website already uses the company introduction, mission, vision, six service descriptions, 14 course names, office address, two emails, two phone numbers, and Monday–Saturday business days from the supplied PDF. The items below would complete the remaining sections.

## Home and About

- [ ] **Leadership profiles.** Send up to two profiles for the home page and identify the lead profile for About. For each person, include their public name, title, specialisation, a short bio, a high-resolution portrait and approved credentials or previous roles. If the leadership cards should show metrics, supply the figures and what they measure, such as years of experience or completed projects.
- [ ] **Company timeline.** Send the founding date, founding story and up to six milestones, with the month or year and a short description for each. Include service launches, institutional programmes or other achievements you want featured.
- [ ] **Institutional experience.** Identify which engagements can be described on About, Services and the investigation page. For each, share the organisation, relationship type, work performed and approved outcome or summary.
- [ ] **About photography.** Send a landscape photo of a real evidence review, forensic lab or multidisciplinary consultation, plus a photo of an institutional training session or engagement. Include captions and identify anyone who may be named.

## Services and cybercrime investigation

- [ ] **Scope and deliverables.** Confirm what the six services cover in practice, including examples of matters accepted, outputs clients receive, and any scope limits. For forensics and cybersecurity, list any tools, standards, accreditations or audit types you want named, with the exact public wording. For techno-legal work, confirm the scope of expert opinions and witness testimony. For advisory and institutional training, share typical engagement and programme formats.
- [ ] **Process and FAQ.** Confirm the steps from enquiry and scoping through investigation, reporting and follow-up, including what a client should prepare. Provide guidance on typical turnaround, urgent support, geographic coverage, whether individuals can engage Cyber-I and how evidence is transferred securely.
- [ ] **Investigation specialist.** Send the featured specialist’s name, role, biography, investigation background, verified credentials and portrait.
- [ ] **Service photography and case studies.** Send approved photos of an investigator reviewing evidence, a forensic workstation or evidence media, devices used in a controlled examination and an institutional investigation or training session. For any publishable case study, supply the situation, work performed, deliverables and outcome. Provide a redacted version when the case contains sensitive details.

## Courses

- [ ] **Details for all 14 courses.** For each, send a short catalogue summary, intended learners, prerequisites, learning outcomes, topics, syllabus or modules, practical exercises and any tools participants need. Include the official course code and level if you use them.
- [ ] **Schedule and enrolment.** For each course, provide duration, delivery mode, venue or online platform, upcoming cohort dates, fees, enrolment steps and the enquiry or registration contact. Provide any course brochure or enrolment link you want published.
- [ ] **Certification details.** For CEH and CHFI, confirm the certifying body, Cyber-I’s relationship to it, and whether the fee includes an exam, voucher or certificate. For capsule courses, confirm whether participants receive a completion certificate.
- [ ] **Faculty and course images.** Provide instructor names, short bios, qualifications, course assignments, portraits and approved course cover images.
- [ ] **Earlier course listings.** Confirm whether Basic Course in Cyber Investigation, Advanced Course in Cyber Forensics and Advanced Course in Cyber Law & Digital Evidence should also appear in the catalogue.

## Clients, testimonials and gallery

- [ ] **Client logo wall.** Provide the approved public name, sector and relationship type for each client or partner, with a high-resolution logo and permission to show it. Share any preferred order for the logos.
- [ ] **Testimonials.** Send at least one featured quote and up to two supporting quotes for the existing layout. Include the approved wording, speaker name, title, organisation, relevant service or course, and permission to publish. Provide the agreed attribution if a quote should be anonymous.
- [ ] **Gallery.** Choose one featured photo and send the photos you want in the gallery grid. For each, include event title, category, date, location, caption, photo credit and permission to publish. The PDF mentions training programmes, workshops, conferences, awareness sessions, forensic demonstrations, guest lectures and collaborative engagements.

## Insights

- [ ] **Launch articles.** Send one featured article and any additional articles for the listing. Include the title, summary, body, category, byline, publication date, cover image, image description and credit. Provide approved wording and imagery for case studies.

## Contact and legal pages

- [ ] **Hours and routing.** Confirm opening and closing times in IST, the inbox that should receive website enquiries, and which contact is best for services, training, urgent incidents and partnerships. Confirm which form fields must be required.
- [ ] **Incident reporting.** If you want a dedicated WhatsApp or urgent reporting button, provide the official number or link, monitored hours and the approved instructions for initial contact and secure evidence handover.
- [ ] **Privacy policy and terms.** Supply the approved Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions text, effective dates, legal entity details and privacy contact. The privacy copy should address how enquiries and submitted contact information are handled.
